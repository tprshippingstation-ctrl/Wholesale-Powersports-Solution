How it works

Three steps, start to finish. Most dealers are done submitting a unit in under five minutes.

  1. 1

    Submit your inventory

    List every unit you're looking to move: year, make, model, mileage, title status, and condition. Add a VIN and we'll try to fill in the basics for you automatically. A few photos help us quote faster, but nothing here takes long.

  2. 2

    Get an offer

    We review what you've sent and reply by email with a wholesale offer for each unit, usually the same day.

  3. 3

    We pick up and pay

    Accept an offer and we arrange transportation, paying you in cash at pickup, typically within 24–48 hours. No consignment, no waiting on a check, no listing to manage.
